# Sources

Written in our own words. Dates are when the fact was read.

- Valve, Steamworks documentation: Steam Playtest. Free feature; runs as a separate app;
  testers picked at random from signups, in batches; keys; no charging for access; "collecting feedback from players is up to you"; no effect on Next Fest
  eligibility. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/playtest (read by Manalens 19 and 24 Nov 2026)
- Steve Bromley, "How to write a playtest survey", Games User Research, 12 Jan 2022: keep it short,
  ask about past behaviour, not future. https://gamesuserresearch.com/how-to-write-a-playtest-survey/
- Jakob Nielsen, "Why You Only Need to Test with 5 Users", Nielsen Norman Group, 18 Mar 2000:
  a first study with 5 participants found 85% of the usability problems.
  https://www.nngroup.com/articles/why-you-only-need-to-test-with-5-users/ (read 26 Nov 2026)
- Nielsen Norman Group, "Thinking Aloud: The #1 Usability Tool", 2012: think-aloud method and
  neutral prompts.
- Davis, Steury and Pagulayan, "A survey method for assessing perceptions of a game: The consumer
  playtest in game design", Game Studies 5(1), 2005. https://gamestudies.org/0501/davis_steury_pagulayan/
- Google Docs Editors Help (question types, pre-filled links) and Tally Help (input blocks, hidden
  fields), read by Manalens 19 Nov 2026.
- Manalens playtest survey builder question bank (`playtest-survey.ts`), and the guides "How to
  find playtesters" and "Blind or guided playtests" (24 Nov 2026): survey length, core questions,
  recruiting sources, 30 players per build as a working minimum.

## AI tagging of free text

Used in [ai-tagging.md](ai-tagging.md), via the Manalens guide "How to analyze playtest feedback
with AI" (26 Nov 2026). Each source was read 26 Nov 2026.

- Hamner and Brinkenstråhle Dahlin, "Assessing Gemini and GPT on their ability to classify
  sentiment in video game reviews", University of Gävle, 2025: 10,000 Steam reviews, 93% and 94%
  accuracy on sentiment, weaker on helpfulness.
  https://hig.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1973484/FULLTEXT01
- Krause, Otterbach and Singer, "Delving into Youth Perspectives on In-game Gambling-like
  Elements, using Large Language Models", arXiv, 12 Dec 2024: LLM kappa about 0.55 on
  aspect-based sentiment against 0.76 between two humans. https://doi.org/10.48550/arxiv.2412.09345
- Dutta and others, "Mining Player Experience Trends From Game Reviews Using Large Language
  Models", CHI 2026, 13 Apr 2026: human codebook from 200 reviews, GPT-4o comparable to a human
  coder. https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3772318.3790760
- Yang and Xiao, "Deciphering the Babel of Play", arXiv, 19 Sep 2026: Steam reviews in 17
  languages; all 558 quotes found; 95.2% of code and quote pairs accurate.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.23104
- Gilardi, Alizadeh and Kubli, "ChatGPT outperforms crowd-workers for text-annotation tasks",
  arXiv, 27 Mar 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15056
- Pangakis, Wolken and Fasching, "Automated annotation with generative AI requires validation",
  arXiv, 31 May 2023: results depend on dataset and task; validate against human labels.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00176
- Anthropic, Claude documentation: Reduce hallucinations (extract direct quotes; retract a claim
  without a quote).
  https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/test-and-evaluate/strengthen-guardrails/reduce-hallucinations
- European Commission, Data protection explained (names, emails and online identifiers are
  personal data; re-identifiable pseudonymised data still counts).
  https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/data-protection-explained_en
- Manalens practice in the same guide: 30 to 50 comments read to write 8 to 12 codes, a 50
  comment hand check, 20 or more testers before comparing tag shares, about 50 comments before AI
  helps.
