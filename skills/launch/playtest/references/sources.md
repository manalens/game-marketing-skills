# Sources

Written in our own words. Dates are when the fact was read.

- Valve, Steamworks documentation: Steam Playtest. Free feature; signups in batches; keys; no
  charging for access; "collecting feedback from players is up to you"; no effect on Next Fest
  eligibility. https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/playtest (read by Manalens 19 and 24 Nov 2026)
- Steve Bromley, "How to write a playtest survey", Games User Research, 12 Jan 2022: keep it short,
  ask about past behaviour, not future. https://gamesuserresearch.com/how-to-write-a-playtest-survey/
- Jakob Nielsen, "Why You Only Need to Test with 5 Users", Nielsen Norman Group, 18 Mar 2000.
  https://www.nngroup.com/articles/why-you-only-need-to-test-with-5-users/
- Nielsen Norman Group, "Thinking Aloud: The #1 Usability Tool", 2012: think-aloud method and
  neutral prompts.
- Davis, Steury and Pagulayan, "A survey method for assessing perceptions of a game: The consumer
  playtest in game design", Game Studies 5(1), 2005. https://gamestudies.org/0501/davis_steury_pagulayan/
- Google Docs Editors Help (question types, pre-filled links) and Tally Help (input blocks, hidden
  fields), read by Manalens 19 Nov 2026.
- Manalens playtest survey builder question bank (`playtest-survey.ts`), and the guides "How to
  find playtesters" and "Blind or guided playtests" (24 Nov 2026): survey length, core questions,
  recruiting sources, 30 players per build as a working minimum.
