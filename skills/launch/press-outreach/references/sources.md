# Sources

Read 2026-11-26. Written in our own words. A number marked "claim" is one source's report.

## Timing

- IMPRESS (Ashley Gwinnell, 12 Oct 2022), https://impress.games/blog/how-to-reach-out-to-games-press :
  give press review keys 3 weeks ahead. Used by the Manalens press email generator (checked
  2026-11-23).
- Big Games Machine survey of 150+ journalists (2024), via trapster.gg (18 Nov 2026): 67% want
  review builds 3 weeks or more ahead (claim).
- Manalens, "Creator email templates", https://manalens.dev/blog/creator-email-templates-indie-games?utm_source=skill&utm_campaign=game_marketing_skills&utm_content=press_outreach :
  first pitch to creators 4 to 6 weeks before launch or the demo, a week minimum (YouTuber Blitz;
  Ryan K. Rigney); one follow-up about a week later with news; embargo reminder 2 to 3 days
  before; 289-character cold email body (Rigney); no earlier embargo for press than creators
  (BRKsEDU via GameDiscoverCo).
- IndieGamesPlus (Thomas Faust, 19 Oct 2018): follow up one or two days before the embargo.
- Ryan K. Rigney, Push to Talk, "How to Get YouTubers to Play Your Game", 11 Jul 2025,
  https://www.pushtotalk.gg/p/how-to-get-youtubers-to-play-your-game : the Spellmasons email,
  289 character body, preview with 80,000 views and 6,000 wishlists a day later; most pitches
  overwhelm the reader (claim, one case).
- Mike Rose, Game Developer, "How to talk to the video game press in 2013", 20 Aug 2013,
  https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/how-to-talk-to-the-video-game-press-in-2013 : 116
  journalists; nearly 40% read an email for under 20 seconds, more than 75% under a minute.
- Manalens, "How to get press coverage", https://manalens.dev/blog/how-to-get-press-coverage-indie-game?utm_source=skill&utm_campaign=game_marketing_skills&utm_content=press_outreach :
  one follow-up after 1 to 2 weeks with something new; no staggered embargos (Rebekah Valentine,
  GamesIndustry.biz). Over 75% of 116 journalists spent under a minute per email (Mike Rose, 2013).

## Keys and scams

- Valve, Steamworks documentation, Steam Keys, https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/keys :
  treat keys like cash; fraudsters impersonate journalists; new developers wait 3 weeks after the
  AppID is created to request keys; Release State Override keys generally limited to 2,500, for
  press and influencer access; keys can be banned by batch.
- Valve, Curators and Curator Connect, https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/curators :
  up to 100 curators, at most 5 copies each; no key to resell.
- Manalens, "Game key scams and fake streamers", https://manalens.dev/blog/game-key-scams-fake-streamers?utm_source=skill&utm_campaign=game_marketing_skills&utm_content=press_outreach :
  reply only to the email on the channel's About page; Whisper on Twitch; one key per person from a
  tagged batch; Steam Key Stats shows only totals, so keep your own log. Cites bit-tech (2 Oct 2014:
  a fake YouTuber email got 16 keys from 46 developers) and Game Developer (2018).
- Game influencer marketing guide (Manalens): creators must disclose free keys (FTC, UK CMA).
- FTC, Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising, 16 CFR 255.5,
  https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-255/section-255.5 :
  material connections include free or discounted products and early access; disclose clearly
  and conspicuously.
- Valve, Steamworks documentation, User Reviews, https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/reviews :
  reviewers disclose a free copy; copies for previews and reviews are allowed, rewards for
  reviews are not.
