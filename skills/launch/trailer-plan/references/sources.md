# Sources

Written in our own words.

- Valve, Steamworks Trailers documentation (file specs, gameplay first with HUD, under 10 s in the
  Discovery Queue, muted viewers, first two trailers before screenshots, microtrailer, thumbnails,
  processing queue): https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer
- Valve, Steam Next Fest documentation (a public trailer on the base game page; Valve pulls
  trailers for the official fest trailer, with an opt-out date). Dates as listed in the Manalens
  Next Fest checklist (`steam-checklists.ts`, read 26 November 2026).
- Valve, Personal Calendar documentation (exact date 60+ days ahead):
  https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/visibility/personalcalendar
- Valve, Store and Platform Traffic Reporting and UTM Analytics (measuring a trailer change):
  https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/traffic_reporting,
  https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/utm_analytics
- YouTube Help, recommended upload encoding settings (MP4, H.264, AAC-LC 48 kHz, 8 and 12 Mbps
  for 1080p).
- YouTube Help, Audio Library (Content ID safe tracks, Creative Commons credit, no responsibility
  for "royalty-free" music from elsewhere).
- OBS Studio Quick Start and Standard Recording Output guides (Game Capture, record to MKV and
  remux, separate audio tracks).
- Derek Lieu, Game Developer, "The simplest trailer to make for your Steam page" (19 April 2021):
  90 to 180 s, 3 to 5 s shots, nothing more than three times, no logos or slow pans.
  https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/the-simplest-trailer-to-make-for-your-steam-page
- Simon Carless, GameDiscoverCo, "Game trailers, breaking down a key discovery mistake"
  (19 September 2022): viewers do not wait for gameplay; do not stack old trailers.
  https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/game-trailers-breaking-down-a-key
- Game World Observer, "Common mistakes devs make when creating game trailers" (22 September 2022).
- Melos Han-Tani, Danchi Days and Angeline Era Reddit ads (12 April 2026): short 4:5 cut with
  0.75 s shots beat the trailer as an ad.
- getplaytests.dev press kit guide (12 November 2026): a direct 1080p MP4, 60 to 90 s of raw
  gameplay with no music or text, 3 to 5 GIFs.
- How To Market A Game, IndieGameJoe tips (7 May 2026): post clips of trailers, not trailers;
  aggregators skip games that are hard to understand from the trailer.
- Manalens blog: Steam trailer best practices (18 November 2026), How to make a game trailer
  (24 November 2026), Game ad creative from your trailer and gameplay. https://manalens.dev/blog?utm_source=skill&utm_campaign=game_marketing_skills&utm_content=trailer_plan
