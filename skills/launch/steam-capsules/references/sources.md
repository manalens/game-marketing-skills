# Sources

Written in our own words. Valve pages are the primary source for sizes and rules.

- Valve, Steamworks Graphical Assets overview (August 2024 size change, "Old dimensions are no
  longer accepted"): https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets
- Valve, Store Graphical Assets (header, small, main, vertical, background, bundle; small capsule
  copies at 120 x 45 and 184 x 69; logo should nearly fill the small capsule):
  https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/standard
- Valve, Library Assets (library capsule, header, hero with 860 x 380 safe area, logo):
  https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/libraryassets
- Valve, Community and Client Icons: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/community
- Valve, Event Graphical Assets: https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/eventassets
- Valve, Graphical Asset Rules (in effect since 1 September 2022):
  https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/rules
- Valve, Artwork Overrides (14-day limit for text, localized text):
  https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/overrides
- Valve, Store and Platform Traffic Reporting (impressions, visits, click-through):
  https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/marketing/traffic_reporting
- Manalens size table and capsule maker logic (`steam-assets.ts`, `steam-capsule-maker.ts`,
  `capsule-readability.ts`, `capsule-placements.ts`), read 26 November 2026. Logo layout defaults,
  the 5% inset, the 231 x 87 store list size and the 70% small capsule fill hint are Manalens
  practice, not Valve rules.
- Manalens, Steam capsule art guide (19 November 2026): https://manalens.dev/blog/steam-capsule-art-guide
- Chris Zukowski, How To Market A Game, "Five trends in Steam capsule art" (27 July 2020): genre
  symbols, catch the eye of people who like your genre.
  https://howtomarketagame.com/2020/07/27/five-trends-in-steam-capsule-art/
- Chris Zukowski, capsule reviews (2025, via gameindustrylibrary.com) and a Unity stream (22 May
  2025): hire a professional, secret symbols, the squint test, clean type with no gradients or gloss.
- Chris Zukowski, "How the Steam Personal Calendar affects your launch" (25 June 2026): look like
  your genre at a glance.
  https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/06/25/how-the-steam-personal-calendar-affects-your-launch/
