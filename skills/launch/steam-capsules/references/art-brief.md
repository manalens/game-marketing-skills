# Capsule art brief template

Copy this into `launch/capsules/brief.md` and fill it from the repo. Send it to the artist
yourself. Keep it to one or two pages: an artist reads a short brief, a long one gets skimmed.

```markdown
# Capsule art brief: <game name>

Date: YYYY-MM-DD. Contact: <name, email>. Deadline for sketches: <date>. Final files: <date>.

## The game in one line
You are a <who>. You <verb> <what>. Genre: <genre and sub-genre, as in the top Steam tags>.

## Who should stop scrolling
Players of <2 to 3 similar games on Steam, with links>. The capsule must look like it belongs in
that row of search results.

## Genre signal (secret symbols)
Top capsules in our genre show: <for example a hammer, a skull, a compass, a fanned hand of cards>.
We want <one of these, or our own take> as the main symbol.
Reference capsules: <3 to 5 links or images of top games in the tag>.

## One focal point
Subject: <one character, object or place>. Mood: <two or three words>. Palette: <colours from the
game>. Nothing else competes with the subject and the logo.

## Logo
Existing logo: <file, or "please design one">. Thick letters, no thin outline, no gradient or gloss
on the type. It must read at 120 x 45 pixels and nearly fill the small capsule.

## Files we need
Layered source file (PSD or similar) with art, logo and background on separate layers, plus:
- Key art without logo, at least 3840 x 1240 for the library hero, ideally 3840 x 2160 or larger,
  with room to crop to wide, tall and square shapes.
- Logo alone, transparent PNG, trimmed to its edges.
- Small capsule 462 x 174 drawn on its own: a closer crop, logo nearly filling it. Valve allows
  this one to differ from the others.
- Vertical capsule 748 x 896 and library capsule 600 x 900 composed for a tall frame, not cropped
  from the wide one.
- Library hero 3840 x 1240 with no words, subject inside the centred 860 x 380 safe area.
- (Optional) A simple symbol for the 184 x 184 app icon and the 256 x 256 shortcut icon.
We will make the remaining sizes from these with a script and send them back for a check.

## Rules (from Valve)
Only artwork, the game name and an official subtitle. No review scores, quotes, awards, discount
text, "Wishlist now" or release dates. PG-13 artwork. The library hero has no words at all.

## Test before final
We will shrink the small capsule to 120 x 45 and blur it (squint test). If the name or the genre
is unclear, we ask for one revision on that.

## Rights
Full commercial rights for Steam, other stores, press kit, social media and paid ads. Credit line:
<how the artist wants to be credited>.
```

## Where to find each field in the repo

- One line and genre: README, `launch/` or `marketing/` notes, store page text.
- Similar games and tags: store page draft, design docs. Ask the user if missing.
- Palette and subject: concept art folders, screenshots, the main character's sprite or model.
- Logo: `press/`, `art/logo*`, `marketing/`.

## Budget note

Chris Zukowski prices professional capsule art at 250 US dollars minimum (as cited by Manalens,
2026). Treat it as a floor, not a quote.
