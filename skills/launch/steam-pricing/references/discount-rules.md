Valve rules come from the Steamworks docs (Discounting, Seasonal Sales, Bundles, DLC, Steam Keys,
Early Access, Pricing), read 18 November 2026. Rules marked **ours** are rules of thumb.

## 1. Discount rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Depth | 10% to 95% |
| Length of a custom discount | 1 to 14 days |
| Weeklong Deal | Starts Monday 10am Pacific, runs 7 days, self-serve |
| Overlap | One discount per package at a time |
| Price changes | Wait 30 days after release; cannot be scheduled; reviewed in 1 to 2 business days |

## 2. Cooldowns

Three 30-day cooldowns. First possible discount day = the latest of:

- release date + 30 days (only the launch discount is allowed before);
- end of the last discount + 30 days (seasonal sales are exempt and start no cooldown; themed
  fests and the Black Friday hub are not exempt);
- last price increase in any currency + 30 days (no exceptions; a price decrease starts no
  cooldown).

Valve also says you cannot submit a discount within 30 days from when a launch discount ends.
Example: a 14-day launch discount moves the first normal discount to about day 44. A price
increase on day 40 moves it to day 70.

## 3. Launch discount

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Depth | 10% minimum, 40% maximum |
| Valve suggestion | About 10% to 15% |
| Length | 7 to 14 days, ends 10am Pacific |
| Setup | Near the release date setting, before release only |

Rule of thumb (ours): 10% under $10, where 15% takes off only cents; 15% from $10. Choose 20%
if you want the wishlist email on top of the release email.

## 4. Seasonal sales and other events

| Event | Dates | Last release day to join with a discount |
|---|---|---|
| Steam Winter Sale 2026 | 17 Dec 2026 to 4 Jan 2027 | 17 Nov 2026 (passed) |
| Steam Spring Sale 2027 | 18 to 25 Mar 2027 | 16 Feb 2027 |
| Steam Summer Sale 2027 | 24 Jun to 8 Jul 2027 | 25 May 2027 |
| Steam Autumn Sale 2027 | TBD | 30 days before the start |
| Steam Winter Sale 2027 | Not announced | 30 days before the start |

- Seasonal sales need no registration: enter the discount Valve creates in Discount Management.
  A custom discount on the same dates does not get the cooldown exception.
- Themed fests need registration (deadline usually six weeks before), and their discount starts
  the 30-day cooldown. Valve usually sets the official fest discount to one week, even for a
  shorter fest. One custom discount of up to 14 days can cover two back-to-back fests.
- Curated Daily, Midweek and Weekend Deals: Valve paused new ones on 29 September 2026 and plans
  to drop the curated calendar in early 2027. Do not plan around them.
- Free to play games cannot join Daily, Midweek or Weekend Deals.

## 5. Wishlist emails

- Steam emails or notifies wishlisters at release, and for a discount of 20% or more that lasts
  over 8 hours and covers the lowest priced package.
- A player notified about your game in the last 2 weeks is skipped. Do not put two 20%+
  discounts within 2 weeks of each other.
- A bundle discount sends no wishlist email.

## 6. A first-year shape

Ours, following GameDiscoverCo's advice to raise depth slowly (its example game reached 50% in
about a year):

| When | What | Depth |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Launch discount, 7 to 14 days | 10% to 15% |
| Day 30 to 45 | First weeklong discount, after the cooldown | 20% (wishlist email) |
| Each seasonal sale you can join | Seasonal discount | +5 points each time |
| Between sales, 30+ days apart, 14+ days clear of a sale | Weeklong discounts or fest discounts | Ramp to 40% to 50% by month 12 |

Do not go deeper at a seasonal sale than at the previous one unless results flattened. Plan a
big update near a discount and pair it with an Update Visibility Round.

## 7. Bundles

- Two types: Complete the Set (buyers pay only for what they lack) and Must Purchase Together
  (only for players who own none of the items; Valve suggests it for deluxe or soundtrack
  editions).
- Bundle discount up to 95%, stacks on package discounts. Complete the Set keeps each package
  above $0.49 after discounts; Must Purchase Together costs at least $0.58.
- Up to 3 bundles show on a store page. Setup is free.
- A collaborative bundle pays each partner for its own package. Every new game or discount in it
  needs all partners to approve again.
- How To Market A Game (14 Dec 2022) advises 1 or 2 partner games so the total stays an impulse
  buy. Developers report the biggest lift when a partner launches.

## 8. DLC and soundtracks

- Each DLC has its own App ID; players need the base game.
- Valve warns that DLC on day 1 may make players think content was held back.
- Reported attach rates: GameDiscoverCo (2019) 5% to 8% of base copies for DLC alone; How To
  Market A Game (3 Mar 2026) median 18% for games that grossed $1 million or more. DLC is
  commonly 5% to 15% of lifetime PC revenue (GameDiscoverCo, 2022).
- A soundtrack DLC is cheap to make and gives a bundle partner for the base game.

## 9. Keys, itch.io and your own store

- Steam keys: Steam customers must not get a worse deal than key buyers elsewhere, and keys
  should not be given away free if you do not offer the same deal on Steam (Valve, Steam Keys).
- Early Access: the price must not be higher than on any other store (Valve).
- itch.io: you choose itch.io's share from 0% to 100% (default 10%); payment providers take about
  $0.30 + 2.9%. A $10 sale at the default leaves $8.41, against $7.00 on Steam before tax and
  refunds. Every itch.io price is a minimum; itch.io says 30% of all money spent on the site is
  paid above the minimum.
- Ours: keep the same base price on itch.io and your own store as on Steam. Start sales elsewhere
  no earlier than on Steam. If you want buyers to pay more on itch.io, add a bonus file (a
  soundtrack, an art book) instead of a lower floor.
