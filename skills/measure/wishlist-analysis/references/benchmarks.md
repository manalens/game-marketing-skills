# Wishlist benchmarks

Ranges with sources and dates. One game or one survey is not a promise for yours. "Claim" means
one source's report, not a checked fact.

## Conversion after launch

Two different ratios are in circulation. Check which one you compare with.

| Ratio | What it counts | Published values | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wishlist purchases / outstanding at launch (what `wishlists.py` prints) | Only buyers who had wishlisted | One game: about 2.2% of ~55,000 in launch week; monthly cohorts from 1.0% to 7.4% | Melos Han-Tani, Danchi Days post-mortem, 8 Nov 2026 |
| All week-one sales / launch wishlists | Every sale, also buyers who never wishlisted | Median 0.15 for 25k+ wishlist launches, 0.10 above $10, year to Aug 2025; 10x to 20x spread | GameDiscoverCo, 17 Oct 2025 (re-read by Manalens 20 Nov 2026) |
| All month-one sales / wishlists | Every sale in month one | About 0.22 for 2023 to 2025 games vs 0.45 for 2020 to 2022 (claim, 160 games) | How To Market A Game, 13 Oct 2026 |

Wishlists from different sources buy at different rates: in the Danchi Days data, reveal-period
fans bought best, then creators, then calendar and organic, then showcases, and paid ads least.
To turn your mix into an expected range, the free Manalens
[wishlist quality forecaster](https://manalens.dev/tools/wishlist-quality-forecaster) uses those
weights.

## Growth and festivals

- Total wishlists predict sales (R² 0.72); 30-day velocity does no better (0.70). How To Market A
  Game, 13 Oct 2026.
- About 7,000 wishlists is still quoted as a floor for launch; many indies now aim higher because
  wishlists convert about half as well as in 2020 to 2022. How To Market A Game, 25 Jun and
  13 Oct 2026.
- Next Fest October 2026: median about 60 wishlists in 7 days (GameDiscoverCo estimate, 27 Oct
  2026); top 10% about 1,500 (claim, How To Market A Game, 21 Oct 2026). Games with both a high
  base and high momentum did best (28 of 34 got 3k+ vs 3 of 47 with low and low; claim, survey,
  How To Market A Game, 14 Jul 2026).
- Personal Calendar (since June 2026): 300 to 3,000 wishlists a day near launch for games in one
  person's default top 100 (claim, How To Market A Game, 25 Jun 2026). If the user's launch is
  inside 60 days, the outlook's straight line likely undercounts the last weeks.

## How to say it

- "Your 3.0% is inside the one published wishlist-only example (1.0% to 7.4% by cohort)."
- Not: "Your conversion is normal." There is no published wishlist-only median across many games.
