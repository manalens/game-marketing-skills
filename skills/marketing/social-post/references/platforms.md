# Platforms and hashtag days

Numbers and habits here change often. Each line says how sure we are. Check the platform's help
pages before you rely on a limit.

## Limits per platform

| Platform | Text limit | Media | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| X | 280 characters for free accounts (longer for paid) | Up to 4 images, or 1 GIF, or 1 video | high |
| Bluesky | 300 characters | Up to 4 images, or 1 video (reported up to 10 minutes and 300 MB since Aug 2026); upload loops as MP4 | high for text, medium for media |
| Threads | 500 characters | Up to 10 images or videos | medium |

`check_social.py` uses 280, 300 and 500.

## Links

| Platform | Where the link goes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| X | Test: first reply vs in the post | X said in Oct 2025 that links are not deboosted, but link posts still tend to get fewer reactions. |
| Bluesky | In the post is fine; it makes a link card | No known link penalty. |
| Threads | First reply, or in the post | Mixed reports; test both. |

## Hashtag days for game devs

Community habits, not platform features. They come and go; check that people still use a tag
this month before you build a week around it.

| Day | Tag | What to post |
|---|---|---|
| Saturday | #ScreenshotSaturday | A screenshot or GIF of progress. The oldest and biggest. |
| Wednesday | #WishlistWednesday | A wishlist ask with a clip, for games with a Steam page. |
| Wednesday | #IndieDevHour | An hour of devs sharing and replying to each other (evening UK time). |
| Friday | #FollowFriday (or #IndieGameFriday) | Share other devs' games. |
| Any | #indiedev, #gamedev, #indiegame | Broad tags; use at most one. |
| Any | #MadeWithGodot, #MadeWithUnity, #UnrealEngine, #threejs | Engine communities; they reshare. |
| 1st Friday of June (and Nov until 2026) | #PitchYaGame | One post per game per platform, about 4am to 4pm PST, uploaded clip. From 2027 it runs once a year, in June. |
| 2nd Tuesday of Jan, Apr, Sep | #TeaseYaGame | A GIF or teaser. |

## Where game devs gather

- **X:** the hashtag days above, engine accounts that reshare (#MadeWithGodot), and replies under
  bigger devs' posts.
- **Bluesky:** custom feeds and starter packs for game devs. Follow them, ask pack owners to add
  you, and use the feeds' keywords ("gamedev", "indiedev", your engine) in text and alt text.
  Reach is smaller than X but clicks are real; treat it as devs, press and fans.
- **Threads:** smaller dev scene; cross-post the X version adapted to its longer limit.

## Platform rules for game accounts

From each platform's own pages, read 2026-11-26. help.x.com refused automated reads, so the X
lines come from archived copies of X's pages.

| Topic | X | Bluesky |
|---|---|---|
| Scheduling your own posts | Helpful automated posts are allowed | No rule against it found |
| The same post again | No duplicate or substantially similar posts, on one account or several | No spam or repeated posts that disrupt conversations |
| Automatic posts about trends | Not allowed | No artificial manipulation of features for reach |
| Paid or sponsored posts | Follow the X Rules | Must clearly say it is commercial |
| Scripting the website | May mean permanent suspension | No abuse of APIs or rate limits |
| Auto-replies | Allowed to people who engage with you; no unsolicited messages | No content meant to mislead |

X media: the April 2023 policy targets media "significantly and deceptively altered,
manipulated, or fabricated" that is likely to cause harm. Animations and illustrations are
generally fine unless they confuse people about what is real.

Threads: Meta labels AI content it detects on Facebook, Instagram, and Threads. Its policy
requires the AI-disclosure tool for photorealistic video or realistic audio made or altered
digitally, and it may apply penalties when people skip it.
