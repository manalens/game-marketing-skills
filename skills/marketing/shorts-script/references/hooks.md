# Hook templates

Use these as shapes, not as copy. Every hook must be true in the build the viewer can play.
A good hook opens a question that only the end of the video answers. Fill a template in 8 words
or fewer: a viewer reads about 4 words a second (238 a minute, Brysbaert 2019), so the hook is
read in about 2 seconds.

## Contents

- Caption hooks
- Mechanic hooks
- Conflict hooks
- Series hooks
- Scoring

## Caption hooks (state the loop or the fantasy)

- "You are a <who>. You have a <thing>. <Do the loop>."
- "I made a game where you <unexpected verb> <unexpected noun>."
- "A game where you <verb>. So what do you do?" (close: the loop)
- "POV: you <fantasy in one line>."
- "This is what <genre> looks like when <twist>."

## Mechanic hooks (show it working in 3 seconds)

- "<Ordinary task> is a puzzle in my game."
- "In my game, <object> does <surprising thing>."
- "Did you know you can <action> in my game?"
- "Some <things> only <happen> <when>."
- "Which of these is yours?" (choice, close: "pick it in the demo")
- Before and after: show the broken or empty state, then the fixed or full one. No text needed
  beyond one line.

## Conflict hooks (a true problem from development)

- "In my game about <loop>, <problem>." (the base shape; say the loop and the problem in one line)
- "There's a PROBLEM with <feature> in my game..."
- "<Thing> broke my game."
- "I accidentally made <scary thing> <cute>." (any opposite pair)
- "Why does everyone want to <odd player behaviour>?"
- "Why did I add <absurd thing>?"
- "Players found a way to <exploit>." (close: fix it, or keep it and ask the viewer)
- "I had to delete <thing I loved> from my game." (close: why)
- "Should I keep <A> or <B>?" (ask the viewer; the only call to action in the body)

## Series hooks

- "<Game> Update #N: <one visible change>." (numbered, one a day or a week, never skip)
- "Funny bug reports, part N."
- "Ranking <things in my game> by <silly measure>, day N."
- "<Game> launches in <N> days." (countdown before a store date)

## Scoring

Score each hook 1 to 5 on four things. Keep hooks with 4 or more on all four.

| Test | 5 means |
|---|---|
| True | The exact thing is in the current build and on screen in the clip. |
| Clear muted | Someone with sound off and 2 seconds understands it. |
| Open loop | It raises a question the last second answers. |
| Says game | A viewer knows within 3 seconds that this is a game, and that you made it. |
