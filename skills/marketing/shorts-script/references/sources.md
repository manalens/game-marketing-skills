# Sources

Methods in this skill are written in our own words from these sources. Numbers are the creators'
own reports and were not checked independently.

- GuuchanDev, "How to Get Ahead of 96% of Game Devs (Using Shorts)": the Caption, Mechanic and
  Conflict formats; say "my game" early; no spoken call to action. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=20TQ7-q2x04
- Dev Dream Pod with Blake Gocey, guest Tim Bee, "First Time Dev Drops Marketing Secrets" (Aug 2026):
  hook first, open and close loops, views are not wishlists.
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HyUCpljXfVE
- Jerbob (YouTube, @Jerbob): Shorts as one running story about one game; problem and mystery
  titles such as "pumpkins broke my game." and "there's a PROBLEM with the evil route in my game...".
- Bite Me Games (YouTube, @BiteMeGames): marketing is volume and repetition, "more shots on goal";
  reuse trailers and clips for each beat.
- Indie Game Joe via 80.lv (Aug 2026): about 1.5 seconds to hook; real gameplay over cinematics.
  https://80.lv/articles/the-secrets-to-viral-success-in-game-marketing-according-to-indie-game-joe
- How To Market A Game (Chris Zukowski), "How to go viral on TikTok": explain the hook within
  3 seconds; measure Steam traffic after the spike. https://howtomarketagame.com/2022/02/14/how-to-go-viral-on-tiktok/
- PastLifeRPG and Pixel_Formation (YouTube): one fixed series format repeated with new content.
- The authors' own marketing notes for their cozy game: the weekly mix, the scoring test, the
  do-not-say list, the TikTok warm-up.
- How To Market A Game, "Wishlist inflation is real, but they are still important" (Oct 2026):
  total wishlists still predict sales; 30-day velocity predicts no better.
  https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/10/13/wishlist-inflation-is-real-but-they-are-still-important/
- YouTube Help, links in Shorts: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13748639
- Manalens blog: TikTok vs Shorts vs Reels for games; from TikTok views to Steam wishlists
  (Nov 2026), https://manalens.dev/blog?utm_source=skill&utm_campaign=game_marketing_skills&utm_content=shorts_script
