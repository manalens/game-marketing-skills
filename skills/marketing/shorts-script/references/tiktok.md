# TikTok notes

TikTok changes often and publishes few rules. Each line has its source and date. Numbers marked
"claim" are one source's report.

- **First 3 seconds:** say what the video is about in the first 3 seconds and land the hook by
  6 seconds. 9:16, text clear of the right edge and bottom, a home-made look beats polish.
  (TikTok for Business guidance, summarised by indiepump.com, Nov 2026.)
- **Warm-up:** TikTok publishes no warm-up rule. Practitioners still suggest 1 to 7 days of normal
  scrolling, liking and commenting in your niche on the phone you will post from, then about one
  post a day. (reelazy.com, Oct 2026; trapplan.com, Jan 2026.)
- **Post from the app:** posts through schedulers or the API may not get trending sounds, and one
  tool maker reported API posts losing reach in Sep 2026 (claim). Post from the phone.
- **Sounds:** business accounts get only the Commercial Music Library; personal accounts see all
  sounds. Your own soundtrack works on both. (TikTok help, Nov 2026.)
- **Bio link:** needs 1,000 followers or a business account. Until then, put the game name on
  screen and in the caption: "search <name> on Steam".
- **No straight reposts:** since the Community Guidelines of 24 Sep 2026, reused material with
  nothing new is not eligible for the For You feed. Re-cut each time.
- **Disclosure:** turn on the "Your brand" commercial content setting for posts about your own
  game. (TikTok help, via zeroraised.com, Nov 2026.)
- **AI label:** required for realistic AI images, audio, and video; encouraged for other content
  fully generated or significantly edited with AI. Turn on the AI-generated content setting
  under More options before posting. TikTok labels its own AI effects and C2PA-tagged files
  itself, and you cannot remove that label. The setting does not affect distribution for posts
  that follow the Community Guidelines. (TikTok Support, AI-generated content, read 2026-11-26.)
- **Length:** short loops still win most of the time, but a 1 minute+ video explaining the core
  mechanic got one studio about 100k views and 2,000 wishlists overnight (acorngames.gg, Aug 2025,
  claim). Test both.
- **Re-cut before you give up:** The Matriarch's trailer only took off on TikTok after a re-cut
  with shorter shots sped up to 130% and shorter text (How To Market A Game, Feb 2022).

Last verified 2026-11-26.
